# Figma Samples - Form Showcase

## Included cases
- `中台表单` form-with-table sample.
- `H-ZERO 流光蓝数据录入` data-entry showcase.
- `H-ZERO 流光蓝表单输入框规格` input control showcase.
- `H-ZERO 流光蓝表单默认配置` default form state showcase.

## Shared takeaways
- These are component/showcase nodes, not business pages.
- They help confirm layout groups, control families, and visual states.
- They do not confirm route, permission, field contract, LOV, lookup, upload, or submit protocol.

## PageSchema hints
- Use `form`, `formWithTable`, `tabs`, `card`, `fieldGroup`, and `table` candidates only as visual structure.
- Mark labels like `Label`, `Button`, and `Please enter` as placeholders, not business fields.
- Put all field names, validation, options, and submit protocol into `missing`.
